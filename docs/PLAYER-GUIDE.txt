LANTERN ATLAS - v0.3

Run LanternAtlas.exe. This is an offline standalone Windows game.

WASD / arrows: walk      E: interact
B: treasure atlas       I: satchel / equip gear
J: commission journal   Esc: pause/close   F1: help
M: discovered map       F2: live camera tuning
Walk through interior doorways to leave; no E press is needed to exit.
Battle: 1 Attack, 2 Spark, 3 Mend, 4 Guard, 5 Flee.

FIRST EXPEDITION
1. Walk north from the fountain to the bulletin board. Press E.
2. Accept 'A light below the hill' to receive your first permanent map.
3. Accept the other two commissions too.
4. Enter Brass & Blade, approach the counter and buy a copper sabre.
5. Press I and equip it. The inn offers free rest; the item shop sells salves.
6. Press B, select your chart, and begin the expedition.
7. Walk into visible monsters to battle. E uses stairs and opens chests.
8. Gold stairs descend; blue stairs ascend. Chests begin on floor three.
9. Reach the keeper chamber after floor three. Defeat it for another map.
10. Return home, rest, and turn in commissions at the physical board.

Your old maps never disappear. Revisit them to find the same layouts and
chest ranks. Explored maps remain recorded. Chest contents and keepers reset
on each expedition. Roaming monsters appear nearby and leave distant areas;
their temporary positions are not permanent parts of your map.
Use favourites and notes to remember useful maps. The optional seed desk
lets you add experimental charts by seed + final quality or share code.

Guard reduces damage, blocks poison and restores 1 MP. Mend restores HP.
Clearleaf cures poison. Bosses cannot be fled, but you may retreat from
exploration using Esc > Return to Bellwether. Read enemy intentions: Guard
heavy strikes, use Spark against shields, and conserve MP. Level-ups do not
refill HP or MP. Companions follow visually; combat still uses the leader.

SAVING
Progress autosaves, with a Save progress button as well. Close the window
normally to save. Loading starts in town and keeps progression and loot.
Save folder: %APPDATA%\Lantern Atlas\
Files: atlas-save.json and atlas-save.json.bak (previous valid save).
Do not run two copies against the same save at once.

Original placeholder world/content and geometry; no Dragon Quest assets.
